My Swiss adventure story

Switzerland is a country of contrasts: snowy peaks, green valleys, turquoise lakes and tiny villages. We drove through the Alps for two weeks and every day felt like a new postcard.

The roads are perfectly maintained, the mountain passes open in late spring, and the trains run like clockwork. Even in summer you should pack a warm jacket — the weather changes in minutes.

«One of the best countries we have driven so far.»
